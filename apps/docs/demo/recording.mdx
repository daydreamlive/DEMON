Press <kbd>R</kbd> or click the **Record** pill in the tools cluster. The pill
walks through *Starting…* → a live **MM:SS** counter → *Saving…*, and a
preview dialog offers the finished files for download with timestamped
filenames.

## What gets captured

Audio and video are recorded in parallel; you pick which to keep in the save
dialog.

| Track | Format | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| **Audio** | Opus-in-WebM at 192 kbps (`.webm`) | AAC-in-MP4 at 256 kbps (`.m4a`) on Safari/iOS |
| **Video** (optional) | VP9-in-WebM (`.webm`) | VP8-in-WebM, then H.264-in-MP4 (`.mp4`) |

- Audio is teed off the player's output node — 48 kHz stereo, exactly what
  you hear (including the LUFS matcher if enabled).
- Video captures the live graph canvas at 30 fps with the audio muxed in.
  If no video codec is supported, recording silently becomes audio-only.

## Limits & behavior

- Soft caps: **60 minutes** or **~150 MB**, whichever comes first.
- Recording auto-pauses if the tab loses visibility or the audio context is
  interrupted; the pill shows **PAUSED** and resumes on click.

## Audio-reactive video backdrop

Recording pairs well with the optional video mode: drop any `.mp4` / `.webm`
/ `.mov` into the `videos/` folder (sibling of `web/` in the demo directory)
and it becomes a backdrop rendered through a WebGL2 shader pipeline with two
audio-reactive effects:

- **Color parallax** — saturated regions drift horizontally, with a punch on
  every kick.
- **Bloom on kick** — luminance-thresholded bloom driven by the bass
  envelope. The same kick envelope feeds the perimeter HUD ribbons and the
  cursor halo via a CSS variable, so the whole frame pulses in lockstep.

There is no preprocessing step — parallax is saturation-driven, not
depth-driven. With no videos present, the demo runs in graph mode and looks
the same. Shader intensities are tunable under `effects` in
[config.json](/demo/configuration). If WebGL2 is unavailable the plain video
plays as a fallback.
