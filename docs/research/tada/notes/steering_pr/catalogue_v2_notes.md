## Why v1 failed (480 directions -> 136 clusters -> 46 passed; 1 too_few: reverb)
1. Dedupe collapse (344 absorbed; drum_machine 66, wall_of_sound 22, enjoyment 21, sharp 20). Every global DSP descriptor
   over the mixed music population lands on 4 to 5 corpus axes: drum density/electronic (drum_machine: onset_rate,
   perc_ratio, sub_db, pulse_clarity, tempo_stability, offbeat_share, key_strength), spectral tilt (sharp/timbral_warmth:
   centroid, hf, air, bandwidth, hardness), bass (bass_heavy), level/noise (loud/noise_floor). Splitting by population
   does not fix music (solo and musiccaps still >= 0.8 for most) but does fix solo dynamics and all of sfx (measured below).
   Text rows with a neutral neg ("plain, neutral sound", "clean studio mix", "studio recording") scored the salience of
   any production word and fell onto wall_of_sound / enjoyment.
2. Second-scorer disagreement (28 knobs with own z >= 2 and n_band >= 2): MuQ anchors are blind to DSP-type concepts
   (bass_heavy: muq moved the wrong way; warmth, muted, shimmering, lush: flat) and to every sfx (weak corr, fallback
   clap_general also flat). Also rhythm words (swing, halftime, ostinato, rolling) where MuQ did not follow.
3. Thin own label (about 50 with own z < 2; 22 sfx, 18 instruments): a PaSST class probability on a 10 s SA3 mix barely
   moves (trumpet 1.26, sitar 1.03, string_section -0.78). CLAP columns for the same instruments move with z 2 to 3.
4. Too few in band (12: sharp, loud at 0; fuzzy, staccato, strong_beat, triplet_feel, flanger, no_percussion,
   wall_of_sound, noise_floor, bagpipes, theremin at 1): LPAPS passes the cutoff by K=4. Not a catalogue problem: rerun
   these with a smaller K grid (K = 1, 2, 4) in screen_knobs.

## Truly missing (not invented as columns)
- Within-prompt contrast: v2 renders 4 seeds per prompt; a label centred on its prompt mean (and the means likewise)
  removes the genre/drum confound for DSP rows. Needs a build_directions option, no new scorer.
- Residualised descriptors (label minus OLS on centroid, bass_db, flux_mean, lufs, perc_ratio): on the v1 corpus this
  takes sub_db 0.92 -> 0.74, roughness 0.85 -> 0.72, crest 0.87 -> 0.72, lra 0.86 -> 0.79, offbeat 0.81 -> 0.61,
  chroma_entropy 0.86 -> 0.52 (hp_ratio 0.58). Needs a catalogue `controls` column + build_directions support.
- Still not written by label_corpus.py: stereo width/pan (mono shards), basicpitch note duration/polyphony, repetition,
  swing ratio, kick periodicity, tempo slope, transient level.
